# Use Case: Finding a Clean Chinese Restaurant for Dinner
### Who is the User?
* **Persona/Role:** Emily Chen, a first-year undergraduate student at UW.
* **Context:** Emily recently moved to Seattle and is not familiar with Chinese restaurants near UW. She wants to have dinner with three friends after class. She has no experience using restaurant food safety search tools.
### What Do They Want to Do with the Tool?
* **Primary Tasks:**
  * Find Chinese restaurants within a 15-minute walk from UW.
  * Check restaurant food safety inspection records.
  * Find a restaurant with meals under $20 per person.
  * View restaurant menus and locations.
* **Operational Workflows:**
  1. Emily opens the Chinese Restaurant Guide.
  2. She searches for Chinese restaurants near UW.
  3. She compares walking distances to find restaurants within 15 minutes.
  4. She checks the food safety inspection records to avoid restaurants with recent serious violations.
  5. She opens restaurant menus to compare food choices and prices.
  6. She selects a restaurant and checks its location on the map.

# Use Case: Finding a Quick Chinese Dinner Between Classes
### Who is the User?
* **Persona/Role:** David Wang, a master's student in Mechanical Engineering at UW.
* **Context:** David is a graduate student who spends most of his day attending classes and working on assignments. He often has limited time for dinner between evening classes. He is familiar with websites and online maps but has never used a restaurant guide that includes food safety inspection records.
### What Do They Want to Do with the Tool?
* **Primary Tasks:**
  * Find Chinese restaurants within a 10-minute walk from the UW engineering buildings.
  * Find restaurants with meals under $15 per person.
  * Check restaurant menus for quick meals and takeout options.
  * Compare walking distances and food safety inspection records.
* **Operational Workflows:**
  1. David opens the Chinese Restaurant Guide.
  2. He searches for Chinese restaurants near the UW engineering buildings.
  3. He compares walking distances and selects restaurants within 10 minutes.
  4. He opens restaurant menus to find meals under $15.
  5. He checks the food safety inspection records of the restaurants.
  6. He selects a restaurant with affordable meals and checks its location on the map.
